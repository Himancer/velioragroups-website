About Veliora Energy

We are a team of engineers, innovators and sustainability champions united by one purpose: accelerating the world's transition to clean energy.

Who We Are

Clean Energy Experts with a Global Outlook

Veliora Energy (Veliora Groups) was founded with a simple belief: clean energy should be within everyone's reach.

We develop, engineer, build and operate renewable energy projects across solar, wind, energy storage, EV charging and green hydrogen. By combining proven technology with smart financing and dedicated service, we help our clients lower energy costs, gain energy independence and meet their sustainability targets.

Every project we deliver is a step towards a cleaner planet, and every partnership strengthens communities with local jobs and resilient infrastructure.

Our Purpose

Vision, Mission & Promise

Our Vision

To be a global leader in green energy, powering a world where clean, affordable electricity is available to every person, business and community.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, reliable and cost-effective renewable energy solutions that reduce carbon emissions and create lasting value for our clients and the planet.

Our Promise

Transparent partnerships, uncompromising quality and long-term support, so every system we build performs for decades.
