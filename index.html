About Veliora Energy

Building a Sustainable Energy Future, Together

Veliora Energy is a renewable energy company on a mission to make clean power accessible, affordable and reliable for everyone.

From rooftop solar for homes to utility-scale wind farms and battery storage for industries, we deliver complete end-to-end solutions: feasibility, design, engineering, procurement, construction, financing and long-term operations.

  • End-to-end EPC and project development expertise
  • Solutions for residential, commercial, industrial and utility clients
  • Committed to international quality, safety and ESG standards
